# YourUnique.cv

Full notes: https://yourunique.cv/llms.txt

Open-source resume agent at https://yourunique.cv. Start from a resume and optional LinkedIn. In chat, share a job (text, PDF, or LinkedIn link), ask a question, or add a new fact. You get a CV for that role, a PDF, and an ATS read (score, area table, gaps). Keep the look of your own template or pick one from the library. New facts go on your profile.

Features on Pro and Lifetime: persona, chat memory, job-aware drafts, visible tool calls, profile updates, bring-your-own or library templates, ATS analysis, PDF export, version history.

Plans (same product; about 500+ tailored resumes a month):
- Pro: $5/month after a 7-day trial (50% off $10)
- Lifetime: $150 once (40% off $250), lifetime access and updates
